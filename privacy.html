PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 14 November 2026

INTRODUCTION

HOTCATPICS ("we", "us", "our") is based in Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India. We are the Data Fiduciary responsible for your personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

This policy explains what information we collect when you use the HOTCATPICS website (the "Service"), including the design terminal, presets, inventory and checkout pages, how we use it, and your rights. By using the Service or placing an order you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the Service.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Order information. When you submit an order we collect your email address, delivery address (street, apartment, city, state, PIN code, country) and the items you ordered (case model, case colour, stickers and their positions). This is collected directly from you at checkout and stored on our server.

Payment information. Card details typed into the checkout form are checked in your browser only. We do not store card numbers, expiry dates or CVC codes.

Presets and likes. When you tap SHARE TO PRESET, your design (case, background colour, stickers and coordinates) is published so other visitors can view, like and order it. Presets do not contain your name, email or address. Likes are counted so the LIKED counter and preset ranking work.

Browser storage. We save your current design, presets, likes and past orders in your browser's local storage so they are still there when you return. This data stays on your device until you clear your browser data. We do not currently use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, session replay or third-party analytics.

Support messages. If you contact us by email or Instagram, we keep the messages and the details you share (such as your name, handle or order ID) so we can help you.

HOW WE USE INFORMATION

  • To process, produce and deliver your order and calculate delivery charges from your PIN code.
  • To contact you about your order, including confirmations and shipping updates.
  • To send updates about new drops if you tick the subscribe box at checkout. You can opt out at any time.
  • To show shared presets and rank them by likes and orders.
  • To provide customer support, prevent misuse and keep the Service working.

PUBLIC PRESETS

Shared presets and like counts are delivered through a public relay service, so anything inside a shared preset is visible to anyone. Do not put personal or sensitive information into a preset.

SHARING

We do not sell your personal data. We share only what is needed with the delivery and courier partners that ship your order, and with the services that host and run the Service. They may use the data only to provide those services to us. We may also disclose information when required by law, such as a court order or government direction.

Some service providers may be located outside India. Where data is transferred outside India, we do so in line with the DPDP Act and other applicable law.

RETENTION

We keep order information for as long as needed to fulfil your order, provide support, resolve disputes, and meet tax, GST and accounting record-keeping requirements under Indian law. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

YOUR RIGHTS (DPDP ACT)

  • Access: ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who it has been shared with.
  • Correction and erasure: ask us to correct, complete, update or delete your data, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw any consent you gave, such as the subscribe box, at any time.
  • Grievance redressal: raise a complaint with our Grievance Officer below.
  • Nominate: nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated.

To use any of these rights, email us at satyekspace@gmail.com. We will verify your identity and respond within the time required by Indian law. Visitors from outside India may have additional rights under local law, and can contact us the same way.

CHILDREN

The Service is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

SECURITY

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

THIRD-PARTY LINKS

Our footer links to Instagram, which is operated by Meta and has its own privacy policy that applies once you leave our site.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy as the Service changes, for example if we add online payments or new features. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest change. For material changes affecting your rights, we will give notice on the Service and, where required, ask for your consent.
